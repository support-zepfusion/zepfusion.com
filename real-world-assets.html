into digital assets.

Why real-world assets (RWA)

Valuable assets, hard to trust from outside

Indian property, film and OTT revenue, carbon credits and handicrafts hold real value. But they are hard to verify, hard to split and hard to invest in with small amounts. Most buyers still depend on paper records and the developer's or producer's word.

🔍

Hard to verify

Was the booking money used for this project? Is the certificate genuine? Buyers often cannot check for themselves.

✂️

Hard to split

A flat, a film's OTT income or a batch of credits is one large asset. Sharing it fairly among many people needs a record everyone accepts.

🪙

Hard to enter in small amounts

Without clear records and splits, smaller investors and investors abroad are left out.

📜

Paper and promises

Records sit in files and spreadsheets held by one party. Unverified certificates and missing payouts are hard to catch in time.

Why blockchain

One shared record that no single party controls

Blockchain is not needed for everything. We use it only where several parties who don't fully trust each other share money and records: buyers, developers, producers, professionals, bankers and investors.

Every rupee on one record

Each escrow deposit, milestone release and payout is written to a shared, tamper-proof record. No one can quietly change it later.

Signed, checkable certificates

Architect, engineer and CA certificates are digitally signed and checked before money moves, so unverified certificates are stopped.

Rules enforced by code

Smart contracts apply the agreed rules automatically. For example, no withdrawals from a project's escrow after the occupancy certificate.

Anyone can verify

A buyer, banker, regulator or investor abroad can check the same record without relying on any one party's word.

Fair film revenue splits

Revenue from each stream, such as OTT, satellite and theatrical, is split as agreed after audited receipts, with every payout recorded.

Carbon and craft provenance

A carbon credit marked retired can never move again, and a craft piece's maker and origin follow it from artisan to buyer.

Simple signing for everyone

Aadhaar eSign and phone passkeys let people who aren't comfortable with technology sign safely, with no crypto wallet to manage.

Ready for tokenised units

Because records already live on-chain, tokenised units can be offered when regulation allows, for example through GIFT City.

Our principles

Records first, for every asset

Whether it is a property record, a rights schedule, a carbon registry entry, a craft certificate or a warehouse receipt, the same four rules apply.

📄

Each record stays its own record

One token mirrors one record. Different records, with different owners and rules, are never merged into a single token.

🏛️

The official source stays the source of truth

The registry, certifier or issuing office keeps the original. A token changes only after that source confirms.

🚪

No one approves their own file

The person who asks, the person who approves and the person who pays are always different.

🧾

Every step leaves an audit trail

Every request, sign-off, transfer and payout is logged, including the ones that were refused.

The shared core

Built once, reused by every use case

Each use case plugs into the same core and adds only its own rules. Adding a new one needs no change to the core.

Identity (KYC)

Checks who a person is and what they may hold. Country-specific fields sit in a separate India pack.

Asset registry

One list of every record: its use case, a fingerprint of the official document, its status and its issuer.

Transfer rules

Before anything moves: is the holder verified, is it locked, is it the right class, is anyone on a deny list?

Official sign-offs

Named officials and professionals confirm a step before it completes: land office, engineer, CA, auditor, verifier.

Request → sign-off → claim

A transfer is requested, waits for the sign-off, and only then completes. Official waits stay, and stay visible.

Milestone escrow

Funds are released only when the required certificates arrive. Splits and certificate types are settings.

Revenue pool

Units in a defined pool of income, with payouts distributed to verified holders.

Record cards & debt series

Shared token shapes: a card that mirrors one record, and a bond-style series with class and vintage.

Audit trail

Every action is logged, including refused ones. Rejections are visible too.

Use-case plug-in

Each use case brings its own settings, pages and rules on top of the core.

Real estate

Land records, home bookings, rental units and city bonds, each handled as its own record. Booking money is released only after the required professional certificates.

Media rights: OTT, satellite & music

The record is a film project and its rights schedule. The token is a contractual claim on a defined revenue pool. It is not the copyright.

  • An auditor or collection agent confirms receipts before any payout.
  • Production funds can be released at milestones through the same escrow.
Example figures only
A ₹10 crore film (example), with 30% of OTT net receipts (example) split into 1,000,000 units (example). No real film or partner.
🌿

Carbon

A technology layer around India's official registry under the BEE Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS). We never issue credits.

  • The token mirrors a registry record and changes only when the registry confirms.
  • Verifier sign-offs, retirement requests and the audit trail are handled by the core.
  • A retired credit is marked retired and can never move again, which helps prevent double counting.
🎨

Handicrafts

Three records, each kept separate:

  • GI tag record: where the craft comes from.
  • Craft / maker certificate: who made the piece.
  • Batch provenance record: follows each piece or batch from artisan to buyer, including resale.
🌾

Agriculture

Records only. Never land titles or farmland.

  • Warehouse receipts for stored grain, confirmed by the warehouse operator.
  • Traced produce batches from farm to market, such as organic or GI produce, confirmed by the certifier.
  • Supply-chain finance records for farmer producer companies (FPOs).
What we do not do

A technology layer, not a scheme or a registry

  • We do not tokenise land, buildings, films, copyright, rupees or carbon. Only records become tokens.
  • We are not a RERA registration, a SEBI scheme, a sale deed, a stamp-duty or registration process, or a carbon registry.
  • A token transfer does not replace the Registration Act or the Transfer of Property Act.
  • We do not issue carbon credits or run a carbon exchange.
  • We do not claim faster registration. Official waits stay; we make them visible.
  • Nothing on this page is an offer to invest or an offer of securities.
